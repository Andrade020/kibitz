# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

**Our pledge.** We want participation to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

**Expected behavior:** be kind and constructive; respect different viewpoints and experience levels; give and accept feedback
gracefully; focus on what is best for the community.

**Unacceptable behavior:** harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, publishing others' private information, and any
conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

**Enforcement.** Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this code,
and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate. To report a problem, open a private
security advisory ("Security" tab) or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.
